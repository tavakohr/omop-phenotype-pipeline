Phenotype: Treatment-Resistant Depression (TRD) — antidepressant-and-procedure-focused concept set

OHDSI Phenotype Library reference: cohortId 1009 — "Earliest event of Treatment resistant depression (TRD)"

IMPORTANT framing: The OHDSI cohort defines TRD primarily through PRESCRIPTION PATTERNS (multiple antidepressant trials), not through a "TRD" diagnosis code. The inclusion concept set is therefore dominated by RxNorm antidepressant ingredients (SSRIs, SNRIs, TCAs, MAOIs, atypical antidepressants, augmentation agents) plus a small number of related neuropsychiatric conditions. The temporal sequence ("failed >=2 trials of first-line antidepressants, then prescribed an augmentation agent or ECT") is enforced at the cohort temporal-logic layer at run time — NOT in the concept set. Therefore, for this concept-set generation task, enumerate ONLY the antidepressants and closely-related psychiatric conditions / procedures. DO NOT enumerate the broader psychiatric or substance-use context that ICU patients commonly have.

DO NOT INCLUDE (these will appear as false positives — they're common ICU comorbidities not specific to TRD):
- Substance-use disorders (alcohol use disorder, nicotine/tobacco dependence, opioid/cocaine/cannabis use disorders, harmful patterns of use of alcohol/tobacco/opioid/cocaine/cannabis)
- Other psychiatric disorders that are not the TRD population (anxiety disorders, ADHD, PTSD, anorexia nervosa, panic disorder, OCD)
- Suicidal thoughts / self-harm concepts (these are downstream consequences, not TRD-defining)
- Delirium, organic mental disorder caused by drug, dementia subtypes (these are cohort-EXCLUDE per cohort logic, but the OHDSI seeds include the broad "Dementia" anchor only)
- Anxiolytics, hypnotics, benzodiazepines (these treat anxiety/insomnia, not depression)
- Antipsychotics used for psychosis-only indications (haloperidol, risperidone — these are in the OHDSI seed but they're also augmentation agents)

INCLUDE — antidepressant ingredients (RxNorm; the gold cohort's largest concept group; use include_descendants = true on the umbrella, false on individual ingredients):
- SSRIs: fluoxetine, sertraline, escitalopram, citalopram, paroxetine, fluvoxamine
- SNRIs: venlafaxine, duloxetine, desvenlafaxine, levomilnacipran, milnacipran
- TCAs: amitriptyline, nortriptyline, imipramine, clomipramine, doxepin, trimipramine, dothiepin, lofepramine, opipramol, maprotiline, mianserin
- MAOIs: tranylcypromine, phenelzine, selegiline, isocarboxazid, moclobemide
- Atypical antidepressants: bupropion, mirtazapine, trazodone, vortioxetine, vilazodone, nefazodone
- Tryptophan, St. John's wort extract

INCLUDE — TRD-specific / augmentation drugs (RxNorm; appear as gold seeds because they are second-line or specifically labeled for TRD):
- Esketamine (TRD-specific intranasal therapy — strongest TRD-specific signal)
- Aripiprazole (atypical antipsychotic, FDA-approved as TRD augmentation)
- Quetiapine (atypical antipsychotic, used as TRD augmentation)
- Olanzapine (used in olanzapine/fluoxetine combination for TRD)
- Brexpiprazole (atypical antipsychotic, FDA-approved for MDD augmentation)
- Risperidone, ziprasidone, paliperidone, clozapine (atypical antipsychotics; also seeds)
- Lithium (mood stabilizer used as augmentation)
- Buspirone (5-HT1A partial agonist used as augmentation)

INCLUDE — related conditions and procedures (SNOMED):
- Major depressive disorder (broad anchor; gold seeds include depression-related descendants)
- Recurrent depressive disorder
- Severe depressive episode / major depressive episode
- Psychotic disorder (gold seed)
- Degenerative brain disorder (gold seed — broad anchor for some cohort exclusions)
- Dementia (gold seed — broad anchor)
- Electroconvulsive therapy (ECT) — second-line treatment for TRD
- Transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS) — FDA-approved for TRD
- Vagus nerve stimulation (VNS) — FDA-approved for TRD

Vocabulary domains expected:
- Drugs: RxNorm (dominant — the gold has 30+ antidepressant ingredients)
- Conditions: SNOMED (small number; depression, psychotic disorder, dementia anchors)
- Procedures: CPT4 / SNOMED (ECT, TMS, VNS)

Strict scope reminder: cohort 1009's inclusion concept set is dominated by individual antidepressant RxNorm ingredients. The single biggest source of false-positive patients in MIMIC is enumeration of psychiatric and substance-use comorbidities (alcohol use, nicotine dependence, anxiety, opioid use, etc.) that the cohort does NOT include in its inclusion concept set. Stay focused on antidepressants + closely-related conditions and procedures.
