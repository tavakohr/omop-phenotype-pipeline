Phenotype: Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (T2DM)

Clinical definition:
Patient has an established diagnosis of Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus — adult-onset,
non-insulin-dependent diabetes. This phenotype captures patients identifiable through
diagnosis codes, antidiabetic medication use, or qualifying laboratory values.

Positive criteria — any ONE of the following qualifies:
1. A coded diagnosis of Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus or any of its subtypes or
   complications (e.g., T2DM with diabetic nephropathy, T2DM with retinopathy,
   T2DM with neuropathy, T2DM with peripheral angiopathy, T2DM in ketoacidosis)

2. A dispensed antidiabetic medication from any of these drug classes:
   - Biguanides: metformin
   - Sulfonylureas: glipizide, glyburide, glimepiride, chlorpropamide, tolazamide
   - DPP-4 inhibitors: sitagliptin, saxagliptin, alogliptin, linagliptin
   - GLP-1 receptor agonists: exenatide, liraglutide, dulaglutide, semaglutide,
     albiglutide, lixisenatide
   - SGLT-2 inhibitors: canagliflozin, dapagliflozin, empagliflozin, ertugliflozin
   - Thiazolidinediones: pioglitazone, rosiglitazone
   - Meglitinides: repaglinide, nateglinide
   - Alpha-glucosidase inhibitors: acarbose, miglitol
   - Insulin (all types when used for T2DM: insulin glargine, insulin detemir,
     insulin aspart, insulin lispro, NPH insulin, regular insulin, insulin degludec)

3. A qualifying HbA1c laboratory result of 6.5% or higher
   (LOINC: Hemoglobin A1c/Hemoglobin.total in Blood)

Exclusion criteria — explicitly exclude patients with ONLY:
- Type 1 Diabetes Mellitus (no T2DM diagnosis)
- Gestational diabetes mellitus
- Drug-induced / secondary diabetes (steroid-induced, post-transplant)
- Prediabetes / impaired fasting glucose / impaired glucose tolerance ALONE

Temporal logic (implement in ATLAS — not in the concept set):
- Index date = date of first qualifying event (diagnosis, drug dispensing, or lab)
- Prior observation: patient should have at least 1 year of prior observation
- At least 2 condition occurrences OR 1 inpatient occurrence OR drug + lab evidence
  is required for strong phenotype definition
