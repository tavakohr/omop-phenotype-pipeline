Phenotype: Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD)

OHDSI Phenotype Library reference: cohortId 506 — "Chronic obstructive lung disease" (peer-reviewed reference cohort)

Key clinical points:
- Irreversible airflow obstruction; defined by post-bronchodilator FEV1/FVC < 0.70
- Two main subtypes: chronic bronchitis (productive cough ≥ 3 months for 2 consecutive
  years) and emphysema (alveolar destruction)
- Condition concepts: COPD, emphysema, chronic bronchitis, chronic obstructive lung
  disease, chronic airway obstruction
- Severity staging (GOLD): mild, moderate, severe, very severe COPD

Diagnostic codes to capture:
- SNOMED: "Chronic obstructive lung disease" (concept for umbrella term)
- ICD-10CM: J44.x series (J44.0, J44.1, J44.9)
- ICD-10CM: J43.x (emphysema subtype)
- ICD-10CM: J41.x, J42 (chronic bronchitis subtypes)

Procedures and measurements:
- Spirometry / pulmonary function test (PFT) — procedure concepts
- Bronchodilator reversibility testing

Drug treatments (enumerate ingredients):
- Short-acting bronchodilators:
    - Albuterol (salbutamol) — SABA
    - Ipratropium — SAMA
- Long-acting bronchodilators:
    - Salmeterol, formoterol, indacaterol, olodaterol — LABAs
    - Tiotropium, aclidinium, glycopyrronium, umeclidinium — LAMAs
- Inhaled corticosteroids (ICS) — only in combination for COPD:
    - Fluticasone, budesonide, beclomethasone
- Combination inhalers (dual/triple therapy):
    - ICS/LABA combinations (e.g., fluticasone/salmeterol, budesonide/formoterol)
    - LAMA/LABA combinations (e.g., tiotropium/olodaterol, umeclidinium/vilanterol)
    - Triple therapy (ICS/LABA/LAMA)
- Roflumilast — PDE4 inhibitor for severe COPD with chronic bronchitis
- Systemic corticosteroids during exacerbations (prednisone, methylprednisolone)
- Antibiotics used during exacerbations (azithromycin prophylaxis):
    - Azithromycin

Exacerbation-related concepts:
- Acute exacerbation of COPD
- COPD with acute lower respiratory infection
- Hospitalisation for COPD exacerbation

Supportive and advanced care:
- Long-term oxygen therapy (LTOT) — drug/device concept
- Pulmonary rehabilitation — procedure concept
- Non-invasive positive-pressure ventilation (NIPPV / BiPAP) — procedure concept
- Lung volume reduction surgery (LVRS) — procedure concept
- Lung transplantation — procedure concept (severe/end-stage)

EXCLUDE:
- Asthma (unless confirmed COPD-asthma overlap, then include as separate concept set)
- Acute asthma exacerbations miscoded as COPD
- Cystic fibrosis (J84.x / SNOMED)
- Bronchiectasis (unless as a comorbidity concept, not as defining condition)

Temporal / inclusion logic notes:
- At least 1 qualifying COPD diagnosis in the observation period
- Age ≥ 40 at index date (COPD is predominantly a disease of middle-aged/elderly adults)
- Typically requires ≥ 365 days of prior observation for prevalent phenotype definitions
